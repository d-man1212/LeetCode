## Plan

- **6 chicken days:** Sunday–Friday
    
- **Chicken per day:** 450–500 g
    
- **3 marinades:** each used for 2 days
    
- **Total chicken:** 2.7–3.0 kg
    
- **Saturday:** cheat day / fresh chicken
    
- **Storage:** Marinate and portion on Saturday, then freeze.
    
- **Thawing:** Move the next day's portion from freezer → fridge the night before.
    
- **Cooking:** Thawed chicken → 180°C air fryer → 7–9 min → flip → 7–9 min → ensure the thickest piece reaches **74°C** internally.

# 7. Daily Cooking

### Night before

**Freezer → fridge**

Move tomorrow's chicken portion into the fridge and let it thaw overnight.

### Next day

1. Preheat air fryer to **180°C for 3–5 minutes**.
    
2. Don't preheat with an empty silicone liner unless the manufacturer says it's safe.
    
3. Put thawed chicken in a **single layer**.
    
4. Give it a **light oil spray** if desired.
    
5. Air fry at **180°C for 7–9 minutes**.
    
6. Flip/shake.
    
7. Air fry another **7–9 minutes**.
    
8. Check that the thickest pieces reach **74°C internally**.
    

### Meal

**450–500 g chicken + vegetables + mint-coriander yogurt dip + fruit**
